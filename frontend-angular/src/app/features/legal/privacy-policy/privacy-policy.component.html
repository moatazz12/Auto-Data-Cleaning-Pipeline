1 Data We Collect

We collect basic account information such as your username, email address, first name and last name. We also store the files you upload and the workspaces and comments you create.

2 How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to provide the core features of the application: authentication, workspace management, data analysis and collaboration with other users.

3 Data Sharing

Your data is not sold to third parties. It may be shared only with authorized collaborators inside your workspaces, according to the roles (owner, editor, viewer).

4 Data Security

We use standard security practices (authentication, authorization) to protect your data. However, no system can be completely secure, and you use the application at your own risk.

5 Data Retention

Uploaded files and analysis results may be stored as long as your account and workspaces exist. You may request deletion of specific content according to local regulations.

6 Contact

For any question about this Privacy Policy, please contact the application administrator.
